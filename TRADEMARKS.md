# Name, logo and mascot

The ClaudePress code is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The license covers the code only.
It does not cover the ClaudePress name and logo, or Nini, the mascot (the images in `web/brand/`).

You may:

- run ClaudePress, unchanged or modified, for yourself and your clients, and keep the name and the images
  in the app you run;
- say that your product or service is "based on ClaudePress" or "compatible with ClaudePress".

Please ask first before:

- using the name, logo or Nini for a hosted service you offer to the public, or for a fork you distribute:
  give it its own name and replace `web/brand/`;
- using them in your own marketing, products or merchandise.
